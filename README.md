# Sharma Lab Portfolio Website

This is the official portfolio website for the **Sharma Lab** at Purdue University's Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry, Fort Wayne, Indiana. The lab specializes in computational chemistry and drug discovery research, focusing on protein-protein interactions, molecular docking, and the development of novel and repurposed drugs for multi-drug resistance.

## Key Features

- **About Us Section:** Introduces the Sharma Lab's research focus and methodologies.
- **Research Overview:** Detailed descriptions of key research areas, including molecular simulations and drug discovery.
- **Team Page:** Showcases the principal investigator and team members, with individual descriptions and contact information.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for different screen sizes, featuring hero images and clean content layout.
